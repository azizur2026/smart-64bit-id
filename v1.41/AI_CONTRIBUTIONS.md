## Overview

SMART 64-Bit ID was developed with assistance from multiple AI systems during the design, research, validation, documentation, and release-preparation process.

This document records the role of AI assistance for transparency and reproducibility.

AI systems contributed suggestions, analysis, alternative approaches, drafting assistance, technical discussion, and review support. Final technical decisions, acceptance of designs, interpretation of experimental results, documentation approval, and responsibility for the project remained with the human project owner and author.

## DeepSeek

DeepSeek was used during the development process as a technical design and research collaborator.

Its assistance included:

- Exploring and discussing architectural and technical design alternatives.
- Contributing ideas related to the SMART 64-Bit ID design.
- Participating in technical reasoning and design refinement.
- Assisting with pilot-test planning and interpretation.
- Supporting exploratory benchmarking and experimental discussion.
- Challenging assumptions and helping identify areas requiring further validation.

DeepSeek's suggestions were treated as proposals and were not automatically accepted. Technical decisions were reviewed and incorporated only when they were consistent with the project's requirements and validated through the development process.

## ChatGPT

ChatGPT was used primarily as a documentation, specification, research-organization, and release-preparation collaborator.

Its assistance included:

- Structuring and editing technical documentation.
- Helping organize the technical specification and supporting documents.
- Assisting with explanations of the SMART ID architecture and rules.
- Helping document experimental methodology and findings.
- Assisting with consistency checks across documentation.
- Helping prepare examples, diagrams, contribution documents, licensing notices, and release materials.
- Assisting with Git/GitHub release preparation and documentation organization.

ChatGPT also participated in technical discussions during the development process, including review of architectural boundaries, limitations, empirical claims, and wording intended to distinguish measured results from general claims.

## Human Responsibility

AI assistance does not transfer authorship, ownership, accountability, or decision-making authority from the human project owner.

The human project owner:

- Defined the project direction.
- Made the final technical and architectural decisions.
- Determined which AI suggestions were accepted or rejected.
- Reviewed and validated the project documentation.
- Determined the interpretation and presentation of experimental results.
- Approved the final SMART 64-Bit ID v1.4 specification.
- Accepted responsibility for the published release.

AI-generated suggestions were therefore treated as development assistance rather than as independent authorship or authority.

## Verification Principle

AI-generated content and suggestions were not considered authoritative merely because they were produced by an AI system.

Technical claims were reviewed against the project's specification, experiments, implementation constraints, and available evidence.

Where experimental evidence was limited, the documentation was intentionally written to preserve those limitations rather than generalize beyond the measured results.

In particular, invalid or non-representative experiments are identified as such rather than being presented as supporting evidence.

## Collaboration Model

The development process can be summarized as:

1. Human direction
2. AI-assisted exploration
3. Design and research discussion
4. Human evaluation and decision
5. Implementation and experiment
6. Validation and documentation
7. Human approval
8. Published release

The AI systems served as collaborative tools for exploration, reasoning, drafting, review, and documentation. The final project remained under human control.

## Transparency

This document is provided to make the development process transparent to users, contributors, researchers, and other interested parties.

The purpose is not to assign independent authorship to AI systems, but to acknowledge their meaningful role in the development workflow.

SMART 64-Bit ID remains a human-directed and human-accountable project.
