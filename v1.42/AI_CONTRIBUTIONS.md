## Overview

SMART 64-Bit ID was developed with assistance from AI systems during design, research, validation, documentation, and release preparation. Final decisions, interpretation of results, documentation approval, and responsibility remained with the human project owner.

## Development and documentation partners

DeepSeek contributed to architectural and technical design discussion, design refinement, pilot-test planning and interpretation, exploratory benchmarking, assumption challenge, and drafting of the v1.42 package.

ChatGPT contributed to documentation structure and editing, specification organization, explanations of architecture and rules, consistency checks, examples, diagrams, and release preparation, and to review of architectural boundaries, limitations, and claims wording.

Suggestions from both were treated as proposals, accepted only when consistent with the project's requirements and validated through the development process.

## Adversary AI partners

Google was used in an adversarial role: challenging the design and its claims, identifying weaknesses and unstated assumptions, proposing alternatives, testing coherence across files, and raising objections requiring answer or revision.

DeepSeek was also used in an adversarial role: reviewing the profile against its own rules, challenging arithmetic, test vectors, and worked examples, and pressing unresolved boundary cases.

Objections that were answered strengthened the work. Objections that were not answered revealed weaknesses that would otherwise have shipped.

## Human responsibility

AI assistance does not transfer authorship, ownership, accountability, or decision-making authority. The human project owner defined direction, made final technical and architectural decisions, accepted or rejected suggestions, validated documentation, determined the interpretation of results, approved the v1.4, v1.41, and v1.42 specifications for their respective releases, and accepted responsibility for each.

## Verification principle

AI-generated content was not treated as authoritative merely because it was produced by an AI system. Claims were reviewed against the specification, experiments, implementation constraints, and available evidence. Where evidence was limited, limitations were preserved rather than generalized beyond the measured results.

## Release note

The v1.4 and v1.41 specifications are locked for their releases. The v1.42 profile is Release Candidate 1, cross-check in progress. This statement does not modify any specification.
